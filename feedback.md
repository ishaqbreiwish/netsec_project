Great project proposal, team! The topic is well-suited for the course.


My main suggestion is to expand the scale, particularly more pages per site (I think this is more valuable than rerunning the collection a second time). More pages will give you a higher chance of observing more differences. Along a similar front, scaling up to more sites would also be more likely to find interesting results (since crawling is largely automated, and much of your analysis can likewise be automated).


The related suggestion is to think carefully about how you select pages to crawl. If it's only from the homepage, you might be less likely to find very different sites. But doing more depth-first searches might find more unique parts of a site (or might not). Main thing is to spend some time thinking about what systematic method you'll use to crawl pages on the site.


Finally, as you crawl a site, do make sure you're still on that same site, as many sites have links to other sites/resources, so just double check you're still measuring the same site.



Just a minor note for later deliverables: citations should be properly provided. You mentioned a prior work by title only; you should provide a full citation including authors, title, venue, and publication year.
